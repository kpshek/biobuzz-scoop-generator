How to print

Start with opaque red or blue filament. In your slicer, switch to opaque white at the first raised-digit layer, above the 2.4 mm base.

Competition setup

The plate is 69 mm tall to fit 57 mm digits with 6 mm above and below. Print two copies of each alliance color. Install two matching signs on robot surfaces at least 90° apart, where field staff can see them.
