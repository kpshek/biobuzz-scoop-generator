How to print

Orientation: Stand the underside of the entry extension on the print bed (or, at 0 mm extension, the existing flat entry end). The wedge's underside remains flat at Z = 0; its leading lip is 0.6 mm high. Orient the curved floor vertically, and inspect the slicer preview with supports off. Add a brim for stability.

Suggested setup: PETG, 0.24–0.28 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 5–10% infill. Check the slicer's bridging preview around the M4 bolt holes and pointed side-panel openings. Strong width tapers can create new overhangs at the floor edges. The extended entry has an unsupported free tip in use, so check stiffness before competition.

Shared .json files contain all editable inputs. The generated .stl is in millimeters. The curved floor and side walls taper together, but the entry mounting-column faces remain parallel. The extension does not add side walls or mounting holes. Only the entry-end mounting column has holes. Three slightly overlapping shells are intentional and are normally combined by the slicer. There is no external JavaScript dependency.
